# SmartGroupFilter

**Your dungeon group in one click - without scrolling through the whole list.**
Built for **WoW Forever**. Works for every class.

SmartGroupFilter docks a panel beside Blizzard's own Group Finder window. It finds the single
players in the list who fit your group - and with one click on **Build Group** it invites the
tank, the healer and the damage dealers you are missing.

---

## ⚡ Build Group

- **One click, a whole group:** one tank, one healer, three damage dealers around your own
  role - only the places that are still free, only players listed for one of your dungeons.
- **The most tanks and healers:** the places are filled so that the most tanks and healers come
  - a paladin listed as tank and healer heals when a warrior can only tank.
- **Your priorities decide:** among equal fills, each place takes the class highest in your row,
  then the highest level, then the longest wait. Players who signed up without a role only fill
  what is left.
- **Friends already with you?** The button reads Check Roles and asks them with Blizzard's role
  check first - their roles count, nobody's role is invited twice.
- **Nobody twice:** invited players keep their place for two minutes, and while they are in
  your group. Whoever declines, lets the invite run out or is already in a group is not asked
  again - the place goes to the next player.
- **One dungeon at a time:** listed for several, it builds for the one where it finds the most
  tanks and healers - they are rare - and names it in the chat.
- **Clear invites:** a player listed for several roles or no role gets a short English whisper
  with the invite - "Tank for Shadowfang Keep?"; one listed for several dungeons gets "You want
  to join for Shadowfang Keep?".
- **Always a fresh list:** is the list older than 30 seconds, the button refreshes it first.
- **Not listed yet?** The button reads List Self and lights up Blizzard's eye tab, where you
  choose the dungeon, play style and role.

## 🎯 Filter the players you need

- **Priorities:** one row of class icons per role - drag to reorder, right-click to exclude a
  class from a role. Each row shows how many players fit it; hover it to see them with their
  level, in class colour.
- **Level range:** two fields and a slider, starting at the dungeon you are listed for.
- **Double classes:** one player per class in your group, or up to two.
- **Armor class:** leave out players who wear your armor - by class and level, as hunters,
  shamans, warriors and paladins change armor at 40.
- **Blizzard's list stays as it is:** the filter only counts single players and never changes
  the list. Delisted players never fit.
- **Reset Filter** clears your exclusions and brings back the dungeon's level range.

## 🧭 Right where you need it

- The panel sits beside the Group Finder's player list, in Blizzard's own look; a side tab
  with the SmartGroupFilter logo shows and hides it.
- Your priorities and options are saved per character.
- Minimap button and an entry in the AddOn menu.
- Chat command: `/sgf`

## 🌍 Your language

English, German, French, Spanish (Spain and Mexico), Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian,
Korean and Chinese (simplified and traditional) - follows your client, or choose one yourself.

## 🔒 Privacy

- No addon messages. All it sends are group invites, the Group Finder's own search, Blizzard's
  role check to your own group, and one English whisper with an invite when a player's sign-up
  is ambiguous - each from your click.
- Stores only your own settings.
- Bug reports leave the game only when you copy the code and post it yourself.
