# SmartGroupFilter

A filter for the Group Finder in World of Warcraft: Forever. It docks beside Blizzard's own
Looking For Group window and shortens its list to the single players you are looking for - by
role, class and level - so you can build your own group without scrolling through all of it.

**Status: in development.** Version 0.1.0 filters the player list by the level range of the
dungeon you are listed for; the panel with priority rows and Build Group comes next.

## Installation

Not published yet. Once it is, it will be on CurseForge and Wago, and WowUp will find it.

## Chat commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/sgf` or `/sgf help` | Lists the chat commands |

## Languages

The addon speaks your client's language: English, German, French, Spanish (Spain and Mexico),
Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Chinese (simplified and traditional).

## Privacy and security

- No network communication, no addon messages and no chat messages to other players.
- No dynamic code.
- Saved settings are validated at login (types and ranges), so damaged files cannot break the
  addon.
- No personal data is stored: only your choices.
